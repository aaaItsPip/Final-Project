Owl Prop

Below is a digital recreation of an owl prop produced for the Rock-afire Explosion animatronic show in 1980. Only 7 of these were ever made out of over 300 shows, with the sculpt being lost to time. I absolutely love this lil guy especially since he was so rare. I have plans to one day bring this guy back to the real world where he can live a second life as a recreation and reproduction, hopefully being able to make it an official product with the original company who created him all the way back in 1980.

Owl Prop

Details

  • 12 Inches tall
  • Sculpted in Blender
  • Textured in Substance Painter
  • Rendered in Blender
