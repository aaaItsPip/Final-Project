Below is a digital recreation of a Rat prop character called Ratso. Ratso was first developed in 1978 as a character a part of the Bear Country Jubilee. He is a single movement character who does backing vocals. He sits atop a balcony in the showroom and has his own spotlight. About 2 Ratsos were made with one or two other similar characters also ebign produced with the same design. Ratso is still around today with the original Company and will hopefully be restored someday and be able to move once again.
